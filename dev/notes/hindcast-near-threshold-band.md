## Hindcast near-threshold retention band

For residual hindcast retention diagnostics, a year is flagged as "near the TS threshold"
when the retained annual maximum is within +/- 5 kt of the TS threshold (34 kt).

Rationale:
- the band is narrow enough to isolate years where modest fallback-geometry changes can
  plausibly flip a year between zero-event and TS+ retention
- the threshold is diagnostic only; it does not change classification, simulation defaults,
  or exported validation outputs
