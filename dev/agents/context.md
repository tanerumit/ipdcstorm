From the exported API, vignettes, and tests, the core workflow is:

1. Read and filter North Atlantic IBTrACS track data.
2. Estimate site-level wind exposure and storm-event summaries for target locations.
3. Calibrate annual storm rates and dispersion from the historical record.
4. Apply climate adjustments driven by MDR SST scenarios or direct `delta_sst`.
5. Simulate synthetic annual storm activity and, optionally, validate and visualize outputs.
